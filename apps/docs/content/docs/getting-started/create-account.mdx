An account on %APP_NAME% takes about a minute: sign in, choose a username, and see where to send
funds. There's no password to remember.

## Sign in

Select **Create account** on the home page, or **Sign in** on any page that needs an account.

- **Email:** enter your email address, then the code we send you.
- **Google** or **GitHub:** you leave for their sign-in page, then come back signed in.

The first time you sign in, %APP_NAME% creates your account. Signing in again later brings you back
to the same account.

## Your wallet

Your account comes with its own Solana wallet, created right after your first sign-in. It can take
a few seconds: the app says "Setting up your wallet…" meanwhile.

The wallet's keys are held by Privy, the sign-in service %APP_NAME% uses. %APP_NAME% never sees or
stores them, and can't move your funds without you. To use your wallet in another app, open
**Settings** and select **Export wallet**: Privy shows the private key in its own secure window.
Read more in [Your wallet and your keys](../how-it-works/non-custodial-wallets.mdx).

## Choose your username

Your username is how people see you. You start with a random one, such as `calm_otter_42`, and you
can keep it or pick your own.

- 3–20 letters, numbers or `_`, starting with a letter. Capital letters don't matter: `Maya` and
  `maya` are the same name.
- Names that could pass for the team or a partner, such as `admin`, `support` or `solana`, can't be
  used, and neither can names made to look like them, such as `adm1n`.
- You can change your username once every 30 days, in **Settings**. Choosing or keeping your first
  name counts as a change.

Your username, trades and holdings are public: anyone can see them.

## Add funds

The last step shows your wallet's address. Send USDC to trade with, and a little SOL (at least
0.02) for network fees. Send only on the Solana network. You can also skip this step and add funds
later: see [Add funds](./add-funds.mdx).
