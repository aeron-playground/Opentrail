You trade on %APP_NAME% with USDC, a digital dollar. You also need a little SOL, Solana's own
token, to pay the network fee on each trade.

## Where to send funds

Open **Add funds** from the menu, or from the Portfolio page. It shows your wallet's Solana address,
as text with a **Copy address** button and as a QR code to scan with a phone.

You can send from any Solana wallet, such as Backpack, Phantom or Solflare, or withdraw from an
exchange. Paste or scan your address as the recipient.

- **USDC** is what you trade with.
- **SOL** pays network fees. Keep at least 0.02 SOL. See [Network fees](../how-it-works/network-fees.mdx).

**Send only on the Solana network.** When an exchange asks which network to withdraw on, choose
Solana. Funds sent on another network don't arrive in your %APP_NAME% wallet.

## When funds arrive

While Add funds is open, it checks your balances every few seconds and says what arrived, such as
"Received 50 USDC". A transfer usually takes a few seconds; an exchange may take longer before it
sends. You can leave the page: your funds arrive either way.

## Your wallet stays yours

The address is your own wallet, whose keys are held by Privy, not by %APP_NAME%. Read more in
[Your wallet and your keys](../how-it-works/non-custodial-wallets.mdx).
