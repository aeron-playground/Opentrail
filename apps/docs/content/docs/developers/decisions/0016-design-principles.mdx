---
title: "ADR 0016: Design principles"
description: Color means money, one type family, and lists over cards.
---

## Status

Accepted on 2026-09-24.

## Context

Trading apps are often loud: many colors, badges and effects that make it hard to see what matters.
People need to read amounts and results quickly, and to trust them.

## Decision

- **Color means money.** Green and red are only for gains, losses and the Buy and Sell buttons.
  Everything else is neutral ink on paper, with no accent colors. Gains and losses also show a sign
  and an arrow, never color alone.
- **One type family.** IBM Plex Sans for the interface, Plex Sans Condensed for big numbers and
  titles, and Plex Mono for wallet addresses and signatures.
- **Lists over cards**, radii by role, one shadow only for floating layers, and motion only in
  answer to something the person does.
- **One source of truth.** The design tokens live in `apps/web/src/styles/theme.css`. Tailwind's
  default colors, fonts and sizes are switched off, so nothing else can creep in.

## Consequences

- A calm, consistent interface where money stands out.
- The tokens include a palette for color vision deficiency (blue for gains, orange for losses),
  and a test checks that every text color pair passes WCAG AA.
- Contributors have clear rules. CONTRIBUTING lists them.
