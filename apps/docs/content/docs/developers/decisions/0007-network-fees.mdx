---
title: "ADR 0007: People keep a little SOL for network fees"
description: In version 1, each wallet pays its own network fees from a small SOL balance.
---

## Status

Accepted on 2026-09-24.

## Context

Every Solana transaction costs a small network fee in SOL, and a new token account needs a small,
refundable deposit. Someone has to pay these. Paying them for everyone means holding funds for it
and guarding against abuse.

## Decision

In version 1, each wallet pays its own network fees. The app asks people to keep a little SOL next
to their USDC, and "Max" buttons leave that amount aside. Paying fees for people is left for a
later version.

## Consequences

- Simple and safe: the project holds no funds to pay for others.
- A new person needs some SOL as well as USDC before their first trade. The guide for adding funds
  explains this.
- Paying network fees for people later would need its own decision.
