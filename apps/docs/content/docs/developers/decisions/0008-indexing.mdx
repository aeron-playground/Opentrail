---
title: "ADR 0008: Indexing with webhooks and a regular check"
description: The indexer learns about trades from Helius webhooks, and a regular job catches anything they miss.
---

## Status

Accepted on 2026-09-24.

## Context

The app shows trades as they happen and keeps every portfolio up to date. Polling the chain for
every wallet is slow and costly, and webhooks alone can miss an event or deliver it twice.

## Decision

The indexer receives raw transactions from Helius webhooks for the wallets it follows. It reads a
trade from the balance changes in the transaction, not from each program's instructions, so it
doesn't depend on how a swap was routed. Every event is saved once, keyed by its transaction
signature, so a repeated delivery changes nothing. A regular job compares recent transactions and
balances with the chain and fills any gap.

## Consequences

- Trades show up within seconds.
- A missed or repeated webhook can't corrupt the data.
- Reading balance changes works for any route an aggregator picks.
- With a very large number of wallets, a streaming feed may replace the webhooks.
