---
title: "ADR 0013: Prices from Jupiter, charts from GeckoTerminal"
description: Token prices come from Jupiter and chart data from GeckoTerminal, fetched on the server and cached.
---

## Status

Accepted on 2026-09-24.

## Context

Every screen needs prices, and token pages need charts. Fetching them from every browser would
multiply the requests to outside services and run into their limits.

## Decision

The indexer fetches prices from Jupiter's price API, and chart candles from GeckoTerminal for each
token's most liquid pool, always in USD. The results are stored and served by our own API, so one
fetch serves everyone. Every chart names its data source.

## Consequences

- Few requests to outside services, however many people use the app.
- A price can be a few seconds old.
- Building our own candles from trades remains possible later.
