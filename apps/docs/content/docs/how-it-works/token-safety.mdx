---
title: Token safety
description: The checks behind every token's safety badge, and what each badge means.
---

Every token on %APP_NAME% gets a safety level from three checks, run again every 10 minutes. The
level shows as a badge beside the token's name on its page.

## The checks

- **Verified by Jupiter.** Jupiter, the service %APP_NAME% gets token data and prices from,
  reviews tokens and marks the ones it has checked.
- **Liquidity:** how many dollars sit in the token's pools, from Jupiter's token data. In thin
  pools, a few trades move the price a lot, and selling can be hard.
- **Mint and freeze authorities,** read from the token's own account on Solana. A mint authority
  can create more of the token; a freeze authority can freeze anyone's tokens. Once revoked, no
  one can do either, ever.

## The levels

| Badge     | When                                                                                                       |
| --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Verified  | Verified by Jupiter, at least $1M of liquidity, and both authorities revoked or explained by a reviewed note |
| Caution   | Under $1M of liquidity, or an authority kept without a reviewed note                                       |
| High risk | Not verified by Jupiter, or under $100K of liquidity. It stays on the list with its badge, and can't be traded |

A token shows no badge until its first check. If a check can't reach Jupiter or Solana, the token
keeps its last result until the next one works.

## Reviewed notes

Some tokens keep an authority by design:

- A **stablecoin** like USDC: its issuer creates new tokens as dollars come in, and must be able to
  freeze funds when the law requires it.
- A **liquid staking token** like JitoSOL or mSOL: new tokens are created only by its staking
  program, when someone stakes SOL. No person holds that key.

For these, a person writes a short note explaining why, and it's reviewed like any change to the
code before it goes live. The note shows on the token's page. A note can explain a kept authority,
never thin liquidity or a token Jupiter hasn't verified.

## Stats

A token's page also shows its market cap, its liquidity and its 24-hour volume (what was bought
and sold together), from Jupiter's token data, refreshed with the checks.

The rules are open source, in
[`safety-level.ts`](https://github.com/aeron-playground/Opentrail/blob/main/apps/indexer/src/market/safety-level.ts),
with a test for every case, and the reviewed notes are in
[`safety-notes.ts`](https://github.com/aeron-playground/Opentrail/blob/main/packages/db/src/seed/safety-notes.ts).
