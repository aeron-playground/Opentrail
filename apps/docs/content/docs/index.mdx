%APP_NAME% is an open-source social trading app on Solana. You sign in with email or a social
account, get a wallet whose keys we never hold, add USDC, and buy and sell a short list of
well-known tokens. You can follow other traders and see their trades as they happen.

<Callout type="warn">
  %APP_NAME% is in early development. Nothing here is ready to use with real funds.
</Callout>

  first trade and withdraw.
- [Using the app](./using-the-app/web-app.mdx): the feed, alerts, profiles, your portfolio and your
  settings.
- [How it works](./how-it-works/non-custodial-wallets.mdx): wallets, prices, fees and how profit
